# Business War

Business War is a mobile-first web game inspired by the classic game Dopewars. Instead of dealing in drugs, the player is a business person trying to build a commercial empire.

## Gameplay

The player starts with a $150,000 loan and must navigate the global market to maximize their wealth within a limited number of turns.

- **Travel:** Move between 7 different countries. Each trip costs one turn.
- **Buy & Sell:** Purchase businesses from a dynamic, procedurally generated market. Sell your owned businesses for a profit (or loss).
- **Manage:** Invest in your owned businesses to improve their performance through R&D, cost rationalization, and other strategic actions.
- **Bank:** Visit the bank in the USA to manage your debt or take an additional loan.
- **Events:** Face random events that can positively or negatively impact your businesses and finances. Make choices in risky illegal opportunities that can bring great rewards at a cost to your reputation.

## How to Play

This is a client-side game built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No server is needed.

1.  Clone or download the repository.
2.  Open the `index.html` file in a modern web browser.
3.  Start a new game and build your empire!
